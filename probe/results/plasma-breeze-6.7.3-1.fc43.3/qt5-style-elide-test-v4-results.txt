=== SE_ItemViewItemText rect probe v4 (Qt5, showDecorationSelected isolated) ===
advance = fontMetrics.horizontalAdvance(text)  width of the glyphs themselves
tMargin = pixelMetric(PM_FocusFrameHMargin) + 1  per-side padding QCommonStyle reserves
natural = advance + 2*tMargin                 what the clamped branch bounds the rect to
drawn   = textWidth - 2*tMargin               viewItemDrawText() removes tMargin from
                                              each side again before eliding
slack   = drawn - advance                     negative = cannot fit; delegate elides
elides  = elidedText(text, ElideRight, drawn) != text
diff    = itemWidth - textWidth               regime-dependent; see v3's note
